AI Hustle Cheat Sheet

Every number comes from the platform's own rules. Checked November 2026.

HYPE

AI art on Etsy

$5 printable → you keep about $4.07

$500/week = 123 sales (~18/day)

Rule: allowed, but say it's AI in the listing

Age: 18+ (13–17 only through a parent's account)

HYPE

AI ebooks on Amazon KDP

$2.99 ebook at 70% → about $2.05 a sale

70% only for $2.99–$12.99 on Amazon.com

Rule: disclose AI-generated text or images, even if you edited a lot

HYPE

Faceless AI YouTube

Before ad money: 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours (or 10M Shorts views in 90 days)

Rule: slideshows/scrolling text with little commentary can't be monetized (since Jul 2025)

HYPE

Copy-paste ChatGPT on Fiverr

$5 gig → you keep $4 (Fiverr keeps 20%)

14-day wait before you can cash out

Rule: unmodified AI output doesn't meet Fiverr's standards

Age: 18+ (13–17 only through a parent's account)

HYPE

AI images on Adobe Stock

About $0.99 per download (Adobe's own example, 33% royalty)

$500/week = 506 downloads (~72/day)

Rule: check "Created using generative AI tools"; no real people or artist names

Age: 18+

HYPE

AI songs on Spotify

A track earns $0 until 1,000 streams in 12 months

No fixed per-stream rate

Likely-AI tracks: 10× more likely to miss the bar (Spotify, Oct 2026)

What's real across all six: the money comes from real skill, real effort and real customers, with AI as a helper. Not financial advice.
