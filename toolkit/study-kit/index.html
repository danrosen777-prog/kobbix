The fine print on free AI study tools

The stuff nobody reads. US rules, read on each company's own pages Nov 15, 2026.

ChatGPT13+ Under 18, you need a parent or guardian's permission. That's in OpenAI's terms.
Gemini13+ You need a Google account you run yourself. In the US that starts at 13.
Gemini Notebook13+ Used to be called NotebookLM. Same Google account rule as Gemini. It only answers from what you upload. Free plan: 100 notebooks, 50 sources in each.
Claude18+ Adults only. If you're under 18 you can't use it, even with a parent's OK.

Free plans all have usage limits, and they change often. If a tool says you've hit your limit, wait for the reset or switch to another one. You don't need to pay for anything in this kit. Rules can change: the tool's own terms page is the final word.

Kobbix · We test the AI hype. You get the 5-minute version.

Why testing yourself: a 2013 review of ten study techniques (Dunlosky and colleagues, Psychological Science in the Public Interest) rated practice testing and spreading study out over days as the most useful, and re-reading and highlighting as low.
